# Inbox Processing System

**Principle:** Organize by how items center around your life and impact.

**Three-step process:** Select category → time sensitivity → cross-links.

## Step 1: Category

| #   | Category                | Best For                                  |
| --- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Career & Work**       | Jobs, interviews, companies, resumes      |
| 2   | **Tools & Tech**        | Software, AI/LLM, evaluations, configs    |
| 3   | **Learning**            | Courses, articles, skill-building         |
| 4   | **Home & Property**     | DIY, repairs, renovation, maintenance     |
| 5   | **Household & Family**  | Schedules, routines, logistics            |
| 6   | **Relationships**       | People, social, community, network        |
| 7   | **Journal**             | Reflection, emotions, milestones          |
| 8   | **Finances & Legal**    | Taxes, budgets, legal docs                |
| 9   | **Reference**           | Evergreen articles, inspiration, snippets |
| 10  | **Outdoor & Adventure** | Travel, hiking, trips                     |
| 11  | **Smart Home & Tech**   | Automation, devices, networking           |
| 12  | **Creative & Hobbies**  | Crafts, making, personal projects         |

## Step 2: Time Sensitivity

- **🔴 URGENT** — act within 48h (deadline, decision needed)
- **🟡 TIMELY** — use within 1–2 weeks (opportunity window, review)
- **🟢 REFERENCE** — no deadline (evergreen, future use)

## Step 3: Cross-Links

If an item spans multiple categories, pick the PRIMARY one, then backlink to secondary categories.

## Customization

Map categories to your vault folders by replacing "Best For" with your actual paths:

```
| 1 | **Career** | `vault/career/` |
```

Adjust labels, add/remove categories, or change time thresholds to fit your workflow. Review monthly — consolidate unused, split overflowing ones.
