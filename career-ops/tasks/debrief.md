# Task: debrief

## Purpose

Turn notes from an interview or call into a decision and next steps.

## Inputs

- The live notes or transcript for the stage. Required. Debriefs work from notes, not recollection.
- The prep doc for the stage and the role's scoring.

## Load

- [references/interview-debrief.md](../references/interview-debrief.md)
- [references/coaching-guardrails.md](../references/coaching-guardrails.md)

## Steps

1. Fill in the debrief structure. Label each item as **fact** (said in the call), **read** (interpretation), or
   **open** (unanswered).
2. Check the prep doc's must-ask questions: each one is answered green, answered red, or not asked.
3. Note whether the call validated or contradicted the scoring's risks.
4. Recommend advance, pause, withdraw, or unclear, with the reason. If the recommendation is advance but the energy
   reading was drained, say so plainly.
5. Draft the thank-you note: one specific line from the call, the profile's sign-off.
6. Offer [pipeline](pipeline.md) rescore or status, and wait for confirmation.

## Output

`{companies}/<company>/interviews/debrief-<stage>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` and the thank-you draft in the conversation.

## Never

- Leave the recommendation open-ended.
- Change the role's status without confirmation.
